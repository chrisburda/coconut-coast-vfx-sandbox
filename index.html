Rider boost

Magic mushroom

Grow into a giant rider and shrink your opponents for five seconds. The screen gently ripples without changing its colors. You accelerate with a rainbow trail behind your tires while their tiny bikes slow down. Shields block shrinking, and everyone smoothly returns to normal size.

5 s · you ×1.4 · opponents ×0.62 · speed boost

Coastal barricade

Drop a coral-and-ivory coastal barrier behind your rider. An impact scatters fragments, jolts the rider and cuts speed before control returns.

Stays until hit · breaks on impact
Boost / protect

Rainbow frog

A rainbow frog sends rippling psychedelic colors across the entire view while a shimmering rainbow shield protects your rider. The boost and protective bubble keep their separate timers.

2.9 s boost · 4.4 s protection
Defense

Lagoon Bubble

A flowing ring of turquoise water circles your rider, shedding small splashes behind the bike. It absorbs one attack and bursts into a wide splash on impact.

Blocks 1 hit · lasts up to 6 s
Tire attack

Shell Snap

Fire a snapping shell along the road toward an opponent ahead. If no one is ahead, it fires backward toward the nearest opponent. The shell clamps onto a tire for four seconds, slowing the rider before releasing. Shields and a held spare wheel block it.

Forward / rear targeting · 4 s clamp · 18 m/s cap
Defense

Palm Halo

A continuous band of green palm leaves circles your rider. It flutters and pulses when struck, stays around you through the first two hits, then scatters and disappears on the third.

Blocks 3 hits · lasts up to 10 s
Obstacle

Driftwood Drop

Drop a crooked driftwood stick across the road. It lands, stays where you dropped it, and trips the first pursuing rider before clearing from the course.

Stays until hit · 1.05 s impact recovery
Rider boost

Lightning Bar

Unwrap a lightning-charged energy bar and tuck into a fast electric sprint. The wheels glow electric blue with crackling lightning streaks for the duration of the boost.

1.65 boost power · 3.4 seconds
Opponent mischief

Party Pump

Give nearby opponents a little too much air. One pressure pulse inflates their tires into bouncy balloons, slowing them until the pressure hisses away. Shields block the pulse.

32 m range · 4.5 s balloon tires · 22 m/s cap
Road trap

Tack Attack

Scatter colorful thumbtacks with sharp steel points behind you. The first rider to cross it gets a burst of escaping air from the tires, collapsed rubber and a short slowdown. Tires repair automatically; a shield or held spare wheel blocks the hit.

Stays until hit · 3.5 s flat · 14 m/s cap
Fly / overtake

Seagull Lift

A cheerful flock grabs your bike and carries you above the pack. Sway gently beneath the birds as you glide over riders and road traps, then settle back onto the road.

5.5 s flight · 3.4 m lift · speed boost
